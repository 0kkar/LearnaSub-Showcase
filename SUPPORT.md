# Support & Contact Guidelines

Welcome to the LearnaSub Support hub. We are committed to ensuring your localization workflows run smoothly. Please review the channels below to find the fastest resolution for your inquiry.

## 🤖 Instant Help & Documentation

Before opening a ticket, we recommend utilizing our intelligent support tools:
*   **AI Knowledge Base:** Access our built-in Vector/RAG-powered support assistant directly within your LearnaSub dashboard for instant answers regarding app functionality and billing.
*   **Telegram Assistant(future feature):** If your account is linked to our Telegram integration, you can use the `/support` command to instantly query documentation or route an alert to our team.

## 🤝 Enterprise & B2B Inquiries

## 🎫 Using the GitHub Issue Tracker

We use this public repository's **Issues** tab to track feature requests, integrations, and non-security bugs. 

When creating a new issue, please apply the appropriate label:
*   🟢 **`feature-request`:** For requesting new AI translation styles, export formats, or quality-of-life UI improvements.
*   🟣 **`b2b-integration`:** For requesting custom API endpoints or third-party workflow integrations.
*   🔴 **`bug-report`:** For reporting UI glitches, translation failures, or workspace syncing issues. *(Note: Do NOT post sensitive subtitle data or personal account details here).*
*   🔵 **`enterprise-inquiry`:** For public-facing business questions.

### 📝 How to Write a Good Bug Report
If you are submitting a `bug-report`, please include:
1. **Description:** A clear and concise description of what the bug is.
2. **Steps to Reproduce:** Exact steps to make the bug happen.
3. **Environment:** Your browser (e.g., Chrome, Safari) and Operating System.
4. **Expected Behavior:** What you expected to happen instead.
5. **Screenshots:** If applicable, add screenshots to help explain your problem. 

*For billing issues (Binance, USDT, or Myan Myan Pay), please contact us privately via email or the in-app support portal and provide your 16-character Order ID (e.g., `LSMQ...`). Do not post financial transaction details on GitHub.*
